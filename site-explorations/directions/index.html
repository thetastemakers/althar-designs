Althar · design directions

Seven ways the brief could look.

Same content in each: the opening, the problem, a stats band, the market table and the orchestration ladder. Press 1–7 on any page to switch.

BSwiss signal

Heavy grotesk headlines, mono labels, and one loud colour for key statements.

ESerif signal

Paper and serif, with solid ultramarine and ink blocks carrying the key statements.

GNight signal

Swiss signal reversed: near-black page, heavy white type, acid-lime blocks.

IKlein

A poster page: saturated ultramarine throughout, heavy white type, cream and black cards.

JBento

Swiss type, but every section is a grid of coloured tiles. Modular and visual.

KMemo

A marked-up investor memo: highlighter, red-pen margin notes, sticky notes for key lines.

LDeck

Each section is a full-screen coloured panel with huge type. Scrolling feels like presenting.
